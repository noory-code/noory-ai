# DE-00000004 Role-policy schema for venue routing, exceptions, and handoffs

## Question

Where does the machine-readable role policy live, how do registration, audit, and session context
use it, how are exceptions and mixed work handled, and what stays out of deterministic gating?

## Options

1. **Flat `kind -> venue` map in `settings.json` + derivation at registration + decision-linked
   exceptions + skill-owned split/handoff rules.**
2. **Role objects (roles with responsibilities and members) resolved to venues.** More expressive,
   but every consumer needs a resolver, and no current requirement needs more than kind routing.
3. **Prose-only policy in canon.** No derivation or audit is possible; advisory drift is exactly
   the problem B-00000003 names.

## Principles applied

- **SSOT** — one machine owner: `settings.json` `venue_routing` maps each routed `kind` to one
  venue string. `official/canon/vocabulary.md` keeps owning what venues mean; it does not duplicate
  the map.
- **MECE** — each kind routes to at most one venue; unrouted kinds keep per-item choice, so the
  policy never overlaps with item-level decisions.
- **AHA** — a flat map, not a role model; venue names stay project-declared strings, so no AI
  product is hard-coded in the harness core.
- **Fail Fast** — a malformed policy is an audit error; a policy-contradicting venue without a
  decision link is reported instead of silently accepted.
- **Honesty** — "mixed work" (one item containing design and implementation) is not
  deterministically detectable; its split rule lives in skill instructions and session context,
  not in a gate that would need semantic judgment.

## Chosen direction

Option 1:

**Schema.** `settings.json` gains `"venue_routing"`: an object mapping work-kind tokens to venue
strings (e.g. `{"design": "claude", "development": "codex"}`). Missing or empty → no policy:
current advisory behavior, zero new findings (single-venue and no-policy projects unaffected).
The declared venue set is the set of values in the map.

**Derivation.** `register_work.py` fills an omitted `--venue` from the policy for routed kinds.
An explicit venue that contradicts the policy is accepted but announced as a policy exception
that must carry a `decision_refs` link.

**Audit (only when a policy is declared, present-location items only).** `VENUE004` error for a
malformed map (non-object or non-string entries); `VENUE001` warning for an open item with a
routed kind and no venue; `VENUE002` warning for a venue outside the declared set; `VENUE003`
warning for a venue that contradicts the routed kind without any `decision_refs`. Archived items
are history and are not judged against today's policy.

**Session context.** SessionStart injects the declared routing map plus the two behavior rules:
derive venue from the policy when registering (do not ask the human during normal routing), and
split mixed design+implementation work into separate items with `parent` lineage.

**Handoffs (skill contract).** `stage-handoff` requires every handed-off item to state purpose,
completed context, remaining problem, success criteria, and the explicit next action, and adds
the back-routing rule: an implementation venue that hits an unresolved product or design decision
registers a planning-venue item carrying the evidence and the exact decision needed, instead of
deciding it or stalling.

**Excluded.** Launching or switching AI windows; letting the policy make product, budget, or risk
decisions; hard-coding venue names in hooks, scripts, or audit; deterministic mixed-work
detection.

## Reason

Kind-to-venue is the only routing signal both deterministic tools (registration, audit) and AIs
can share without semantic judgment, and it satisfies every B-00000003 criterion: derivation
without asking, decision-linked exceptions, audit of missing/unknown/mismatched venues, and
unchanged behavior for projects that declare nothing. Role objects add resolver complexity with
no present consumer, and prose-only policy cannot be derived from or audited.
