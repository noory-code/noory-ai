# R-00000014 Role-policy-driven venue routing and handoffs

## Work

Made venue assignment derive from a project-declared `kind -> venue` role policy: a
machine-readable `venue_routing` map in `settings.json`, automatic derivation at registration
with decision-linked exceptions, four audit consistency checks, an injected session directive
carrying the split and back-routing rules, and a five-element self-contained handoff contract.
Released as Stage 0.21.0.

## Decision points

- Chose a flat `kind -> venue` map over role objects or prose-only policy; DE-00000004 records
  why the alternatives either add resolver complexity with no consumer or cannot be derived from
  or audited.
- Kept mixed-work detection out of deterministic gates — splitting is a skill and session-context
  instruction, because "this item contains both design and implementation" is a semantic
  judgment.
- Scoped audit checks to open present items so archived history is never judged against a policy
  declared later.
- Moved the machine policy owner from canon prose to `settings.json` while canon keeps owning
  what venues mean, resolving the residual two-owner ambiguity from W-00000012's vocabulary
  entry.

## Principles applied

- SSOT: one machine owner (`venue_routing`) for routing; canon owns meaning only.
- MECE: each kind routes to at most one venue; unrouted kinds keep per-item choice.
- AHA: no role model until kind-only routing demonstrably forces awkward splits.
- Fail Fast: malformed policy is an audit error; contradictions without a decision link are
  reported instead of silently accepted.
- Honesty: no-policy projects get zero new findings — behavior claims match what ships.

## Context that helped

- The venue vocabulary entry promoted by W-00000012 gave this repository a declared meaning to
  bind the machine map to.
- The `AuditedItem.fields` raw-frontmatter channel let the audit read `venue` and
  `decision_refs` without widening the `WorkItem` dataclass.

## Context that was missing

- No prior record stated which venue should own `feature`, `documentation`, and `chore` in this
  repository; they stay deliberately unrouted (per-item choice) until practice shows a stable
  owner.

## What changes in later behavior

- Registering work in a policy project: never ask the human for a venue on routed kinds; when
  contradicting the policy, record the authorizing decision first.
- Feature work that mixes design and implementation registers as two items with `parent`
  lineage from the start.
- Handoffs must answer purpose, completed context, remaining problem, success criteria, and next
  action before the session ends.
- The next retrospective on this feature must check whether kind-only routing forced awkward
  splits (the trigger for a richer role model per DE-00000004).

## Routing

- Everything lives in the released `stage/` plugin and this repository's `.stage/settings.json`;
  nothing new promotes to `past/`.
