# W-00000005 Archive completed work items W-00000001..W-00000004

## Purpose

W-00000001..W-00000004 are all `completed` (verification passed, retrospective completed,
promotion not_applicable) with no active/review/blocked item naming them as parent and no open
question, assumption, or risk referencing them. Per `operations/artifacts.md`, such items are
archived to `past/work/archive/` to drain the review queue. This item covers that archive
operation, since `past/work/archive/` writes require an open work item with matching scope.

## Scope

`.stage/past/work/archive/` — move each item to `archive/items/`, its retrospective to
`archive/retrospectives/`, and add its row (with the final `completed` status) to
`archive/index.md`. Removing the present copies and clearing `review.md` are present-side
flow edits (not governed source).

## Success criteria

- W-1..W-4 item files live in `past/work/archive/items/` with `status: archived`.
- Their retrospectives live in `past/work/archive/retrospectives/`.
- `archive/index.md` has a row per item with `Final status` = `completed`.
- `review.md` no longer lists W-1..W-4; the audit reports errors=0.

## Related truth

- `operations/artifacts.md` — archive rules and the `Final status` record.
- `hooks/stage_runtime.py` — the archive-intent gate (per-item intent, path/ref matching).

## Verification

`python3 stage/scripts/audit_stage.py` — errors=0 after the moves.

## Retrospective

See [R-00000005](../retrospectives/R-00000005.md). Learning — `past/work/archive/` writes need
both an open scoped work item (registration gate) and a per-item archive intent (promotion gate),
and shell targets must be literal paths, not variables.

## Promotion decision

`promotion: not_applicable` — archiving is record keeping, not a `past/` promotion of new canon.
