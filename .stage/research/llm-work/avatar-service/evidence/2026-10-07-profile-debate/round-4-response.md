### Prototype three, defer one

1. **Three-view portrait** (P11: portrait, conditions, revisions).
   *Benefit:* a connected picture without a personality type.
   *Drop it if:* in the revised P6 test, users can't pick their own card above chance, and they also say it changed nothing about how they work with the AI.

2. **Distinct correction operations with source deletion that reaches dependent claims** (P12).
   *Benefit:* the user controls the portrait without being interviewed.
   *Drop it (merge into one "wrong" button) if:* pilot users almost never choose any operation except plain rejection.

3. **Sealed prediction game.** Avatar seals a guess about the response style before a task. The guess must not influence the reply.
   *Benefit:* a concrete, playful moment where the portrait can be checked.
   *Drop it if:* it doesn't beat the baseline of always predicting the user's most common choice in the pilot sessions.

**Defer: Style Mirror / persona rehearsal** (P9).
*Benefit:* the user can try out a style they want to have.
*Why defer:* it changes how the data is produced. I'd revisit it once the passive core passes test 1.

The attractive idea I rejected in Round 2 (a shareable fixed-type badge) stays rejected. A public gallery of fictional portraits gives the ad pages content without putting anyone's profile at risk. That is a hypothesis, and I'd drop it if those pages get no traffic.
