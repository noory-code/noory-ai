# R-00000018 Schema v4 official-zone design freeze

## Work

Authored `stage/docs/SCHEMA_V4.md` as the canonical schema v4 design and recorded the
adoption decision (DE-00000009). The design was produced through two adversarial
Claude-Codex debates (5 + 10 rounds) plus one post-verdict amendment round, with the owner
ruling every open fork in session.

## Decision points

- Time-first vs space-first vs hybrid topology → official-zone topology (DE-00000009).
- Authorization zone name: keep `past/` vs rename → `official/` (owner ruling, Codex
  re-approval after cost re-pricing under the registry/template-regeneration plan).
- Decision identity across promotion → Contract X (permanent DE id).
- Terminal disposition ownership → card frontmatter, archive index derived.
- Defense triage → three shipped, two deferred with named triggers.

## Principles applied

- SSOT: one topology registry; status derived from decision chains; single owner per fact
  (milestone link on the card, disposition on the card).
- MECE: disjoint root families; single-lifecycle classification audited.
- Fail Fast: fail-closed migration steps, marker-last stamping, closure revalidation.
- AHA: deferred defenses and sqlite index wait for declared triggers.

  discovery that DE→D promotion was never implemented both redirected the design.
- The owner's mid-debate clarification (time triad = thinking frame governing behavior, not
  storage) dissolved the location dispute and became design axiom 1.

## Context that was missing
