# DE-00000005 Split contract, validated venue exceptions, and the open-decision completion gate

## Question

How does the harness enforce, without semantic judgment, that mixed work splits into
design/implementation items, that policy-contradicting venues apply only through approved
decisions, and that work cannot complete on top of undecided decisions?

## Options

1. **Reserved `split` routing value + decision-validated exceptions + completion gate on open
   decisions.** All three enforced by the same deterministic structures already in the harness
   (routing map, decision records, completion blockers).
2. **Semantic audit of item bodies** to detect mixed design+implementation content — guessing;
   explicitly rejected by the review directive and the Honesty principle.
3. **Status quo** (SessionStart instruction only) — advisory drift; the gap under review.

## Principles applied

- **Fail Fast** — a split-marked kind refuses single-item registration with the exact resolution
  action, instead of silently mis-routing; completion refuses while a linked decision is open.
- **SSOT** — `settings.json` `venue_routing` is the one machine owner of routing; canon
  vocabulary owns meaning only; one validation contract shared by CLI and audit.
- **MECE** — every kind is either routed to one venue, marked `split`, or deliberately unrouted
  (per-item choice); exceptions are the one escape hatch and always decision-linked.
- **Honesty** — no semantic detection; "advisory" wording that no longer matches audit behavior
  is corrected everywhere it appears.

## Chosen direction

Option 1:

**Split contract.** `venue_routing` values may be a venue name or the reserved token `split`.
`split` declares the kind mixed by definition: `register_work.py` refuses to register it as a
single item and prints the resolution — register a design-kind item and an implementation-kind
item with `parent` lineage — unless a valid exception decision is linked. The audit reports an
open single item of a split kind without a valid exception (`VENUE005` error). `split` is
reserved and cannot be used as a venue name.

**Validated exceptions (one contract for CLI and audit).** A venue exception — a
policy-contradicting venue or a deliberate single item of a split kind — is valid only when a
linked decision record: exists, has `status: decided` or `promoted`, machine-readably declares
`authorizes: venue_exception` in its frontmatter, and names the work item (`work_item`).
`register_work.py` gains `--decision <DE-id>`: it validates existence, status, and the
`authorizes` field at registration (refusing otherwise with exit 1) and stamps `decision_refs`;
the audit enforces the full contract including the `work_item` back-link (`VENUE003` and
`VENUE005` become errors when the contract is not met). Message-only acceptance is removed.

**Completion gate.** A work item whose `decision_refs` names a decision still `status: open`
cannot complete: `close_work.py` refuses, and the audit reports any completed or archived item
referencing an open decision (`DECISION002` error). Decisions must be closed (`decided`,
`promoted`, or another final status) before the work that depends on them is called done.

**This repository's routing** (covers every declared kind): `planning`, `design`,
`documentation` → `claude`; `development`, `fix`, `qa`, `ops`, `chore` → `codex`;
`feature` → `split`.

**Corrections applied with this decision.** DE-00000002..4 move from `open` to `decided` — their
directions were chosen and executed; `open` was a bookkeeping omission the new gate would have
caught. This repository's `settings.json` declares `"language": "en"` explicitly, making the
recorded claim in DE-00000003/W-00000013 true with settings as the single owner.

## Reason

All three gaps share one root: contracts stated in prose but not carried by any deterministic
structure. The routing map, decision records, and completion blockers already exist; extending
them closes the gaps without inventing semantic judgment, and no-policy projects see zero new
behavior because every new check activates only when `venue_routing` is declared or a decision is
actually linked.
