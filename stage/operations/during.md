# During

This document owns the work-execution gate.

## Gate

During work:

1. Apply principles at decision points.
2. Apply the host project's own instructions (rules, skills, `CLAUDE.md`/`AGENTS.md`) alongside Stage principles; when a host instruction proves wrong against observed reality, register the conflict as an open question with a proposed correction instead of silently deviating or silently obeying.
3. Keep cases and branches MECE.
4. Keep ownership as SSOT.
5. Surface broken assumptions early.
6. Avoid hasty abstractions.
7. Route provisional findings to `state/`.
